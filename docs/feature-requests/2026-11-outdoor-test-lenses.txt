Feedback from the fire safety engineers (relayed 2026-11-02): for an outdoor vehicle fire test, three IR cameras (A8303sc, A700, T650sc) stand at a 28 m safety distance, which is also their default position; they may move further back, but cable runs to one laptop keep them close together, at 1.0 to 1.5 m height. The A8303sc should capture the whole flame (peak about 10 m above the ground); the A700 and T650sc concentrate on the battery, below the load platform between the front and rear wheels, where 15-20 thermocouples will be attached, and the IR video will be used to extract temperatures at the thermocouple positions for comparison, so the thermocouples' pixels should not be too close to each other. They asked whether new lenses are needed, and to see every lens available for the three cameras.

Agreed scope: every standoff lens FLIR lists for the three bodies (the T650sc's five, the A8303sc's 200 mm, the A700's second 24 deg lens); a per-camera and per-lens readout of the thermocouple beads in the image (seen, closest pair in pixels against a 3 x 3 px spot); camera roll, so a camera can be mounted on its side; a flame height field that back-solves the HRR; and plans with the vehicle, the flame and the cameras for the engineers, kept outside the repository.
